## Notes on this format

- Dropped format-only turns (e.g. "output the above into markdown") — not content questions, no expected answer points/chunks to grade against; would need a separate structural eval, not this table.
- "Expected answer points" are the facts a correct answer must contain, not exact wording — grading is content-match, not string-match.
- Each follow-up requires carrying context forward (company = LITE, topic = bull/base/bear PT framework) without restating it — tests session memory, not just single-shot retrieval.
- Turns 3 and 6 are designed to connect (bear case mechanism ↔ named competitive risk) — a good session eval should reward an answer in turn 6 that references back to turn 3's bear case framing, and penalize one that treats it as a fresh, disconnected question.
